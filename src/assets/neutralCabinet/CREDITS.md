# Credits

The cabinet geometry comes from two Visual Pinball X VR tables. All their artwork was left out: the textures here are new (satin black, a smoked translite, a plain speaker panel and generic coin inserts).

- Cabinet body, backbox, coin door, rails, lockbar, legs, buttons and plunger: the VR Time Machine table by JPSalas (VP), JPJ, Chucky and Arngrim (Team PP), VR conversion by Sixtoe.
- Launch button and its housing: the VR Attack from Mars table by g5k, VR room by Rawd.
- The generic start button graphic on the front panel is taken from the Time Machine cabinet texture.

Thanks to them for sharing their work.
